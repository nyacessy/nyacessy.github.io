Selected projects

Explore Sierra Leone

An interactive tourism map featuring 35 selected attractions across Sierra Leone, including beaches, heritage sites, islands and natural attractions. Users can search by place or category, select results to explore their locations, and open map pop-ups for descriptions and source links.

Methods: Manual data compilation, coordinate mapping, category-based symbols, pop-up configuration, and responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and phone.

Tools: ArcGIS Online and ArcGIS Experience Builder.

A selection of attractions, rather than a complete tourism directory. Locations are approximate.

Explore tourism map

Pennsylvania Measles Dashboard

A county-level dashboard displaying reported case counts and cases per 100,000 residents, with interactive charts and county pop-ups.

Methods: Data cleaning, attribute joins, population-based rates, and web visualization.

Tools: ArcGIS Pro, Excel, ArcGIS Online, ArcGIS Dashboards.

Data snapshot: October 5, 2026. Updates are manual.

Explore dashboard

Montgomery County Reported Crashes, 2023–2025

I analyzed 31,670 reported crashes to explore where and when crashes occurred in Montgomery County, Maryland. The dashboard combines a 1 km grid map with yearly and hourly charts and summaries of injury and fatal crashes.

Methods: Data cleaning, coordinate projection, spatial joins, and spatial and temporal summaries.

Tools: ArcGIS Pro, Excel, ArcGIS Online, ArcGIS Dashboards.

Explore dashboard

Maryland Autism Services Locator

A web GIS capstone project helping users explore autism support services across Maryland through category filters, search, and location-based tools.

Methods: Service data preparation, geocoding, and interactive web mapping.

Tools: ArcGIS Pro, Excel, Leaflet, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Service information is a static dataset and may change.
