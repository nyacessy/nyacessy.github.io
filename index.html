I analyzed 31,670 reported crashes to explore where and when crashes occurred in Montgomery County, Maryland. The dashboard combines a 1 km grid map with yearly and hourly charts and summaries of injury and fatal crashes.

Tools: ArcGIS Pro, Excel, ArcGIS Online, ArcGIS Dashboards.

Methods: Data cleaning, coordinate projection, spatial joins, and spatial and temporal summaries.
